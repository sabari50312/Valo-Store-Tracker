# Privacy Policy

**Val Store** · Last updated 24 October 2026

Val Store is a free, fan-made Android app for checking your VALORANT store. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games.

## The short version

- Val Store doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data.
- There are no Val Store servers, accounts, analytics, ads or crash trackers.
- Everything the app keeps stays on your phone.

## Signing in

You sign in on Riot Games' own login page, shown inside the app. Your username and password go straight to Riot. Val Store never sees, reads or stores them.

After you sign in, Riot gives the app a session so it can load your store without asking you to sign in every time. That session is stored on your phone in the app's private storage, the same way a browser remembers a login. **Sign out** in Settings deletes it.

## What's stored on your phone

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Riot session | So you stay signed in |
| Your wishlist | So the app knows which skins to alert you about |
| Alert timing (when your store last reset, which alerts were already sent) | So you get each alert once |

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Signing out deletes the session and alert timing; your wishlist stays until you remove it or uninstall.

## Who the app talks to

| Service | What for |
|---|---|
| Riot Games | Signing in, and loading your store, night market, accessories and wallet |
| valorant-api.com | Skin names, images, rarity and preview videos (no account data is sent) |
| Riot's content servers | Playing skin preview videos |

These services have their own privacy policies; for example, [Riot's privacy notice](https://www.riotgames.com/en/privacy-notice).

## Notifications and background checks

If you turn on **Store alerts**, the app checks your store in the background once each time it resets, and shows a notification on your phone when a wishlisted skin appears. The check runs entirely on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere except the request to Riot for your store.

## Children

Val Store isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes
